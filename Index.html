It was a cozy afternoon in the spring with a hint of sunshine. Just on the corner of road, there was this small group of shops/ businesses called Silver Plaza market.

This market had a variety of businesses such as coffee shops, snack points, beauty salons, a gym, and a bakery.

Aryan was standing just a few meters away from this bakery. It belonged to the mother of his enemy.

She was wearing a white apron on top of a yellow t-shirt, (kind of like from muriel from courage the cowardly dog). She had white hair and a warm smile on her face.

The little dog sat near her, tilted his head to the left and started wagging his tail with his tongue sticking out, he had curiosity in his eyes about what his mother was doing.

"I know you're hungry my baby... Don't worry, mama will give you a treat very soon 😚" said the baker.

"He likes you, he loves to run around people he likes" said the baker.
